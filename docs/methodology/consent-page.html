Participant Information & Consent

You are invited to take part in a scientific study on visual perception, conducted by Amit Gur, University of Antwerp. Please read the following before deciding to participate.

  • Voluntary participation. Taking part is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any moment by closing this window.
  • Use of data. The data obtained in this session will be used for a scientific study and may be published in anonymized, aggregated form in scientific journals and presentations.
  • Anonymity. No identifying information is collected. Your responses, your age and gender, and technical details of your screen are stored under an anonymous participant code only, which is removed from the dataset before publication.
  • Risks. There are no known risks beyond those of ordinary computer use.
  • Ethical approval. This study has been approved by the Ethics Committee for the Social Sciences and Humanities of the University of Antwerp (PENDING APPROVAL – reference number and date will be added later).
  • Contact. Questions may be addressed to Amit.Gur@UAntwerpen.be.
